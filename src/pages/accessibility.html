Accessibility Statement

Everyone should be able to use this website, including older adults, caregivers and people who use assistive technology.

Our commitment

We are working to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

What we have done

  • Large, readable text that scales with your browser settings
  • Strong color contrast between text and backgrounds
  • A skip link and full keyboard navigation, with visible focus indicators
  • A simple mobile menu and large tap targets
  • Descriptive labels, instructions and error messages on every form
  • Text descriptions for photographs
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences

Need help?

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, please email delane@delanesnails.com or visit our Contact page. We will help, and we welcome your suggestions.
