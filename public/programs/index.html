Programs and scholarships

Training for a career. Mentors for the journey.

Scholarship-supported vocational training, mentorship and classes for women and single parents.

How to Apply

Vocational training

Scholarships toward a professional license

DNNC Steps to Success offers scholarships and training for women and single parents who want to become manicurists or cosmetologists. The program works in partnership with Delane’s Natural Nail Care, where participants practice in a working salon.

  • Manicurist and cosmetology pathways
  • Medical pedicurist training, a specialty that can lead to a thriving wage in the Bay Area
  • Internships and job-readiness training
  • State license exam preparation
  • Support toward job placement and, for some, salon ownership

Mentorship and support

People who believe in you

  • Mentoring and professional guidance
  • Mental wellness support
  • Classes in financial literacy, etiquette, nutrition, parenting and more
  • Help in emergencies, such as utilities and household needs
  • A vehicle giveaway for a participant, as part of our community events

The program lasts one year, and our family stays open to those who need us. Once a Steps to Success family member, always a Steps to Success family member.

The path

What the journey looks like

Cohorts have traditionally started in the spring and fall.

  1. Enroll and train
  2. Practice through internship
  3. Prepare for licensure
  4. Launch a career

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply?

Start with a scholarship inquiry or an email to us. We will explain the current application steps and answer your questions.

Is there a cost to join?

The program provides scholarships toward training, and classes and mentorship are offered to participants at no charge. Ask us about the current scholarship details when you get in touch.

When does the next cohort begin?

Cohorts have traditionally begun in the spring and fall. Contact us for the next start date.
