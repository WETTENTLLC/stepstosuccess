Our story and leadership

A mission built from lived experience

How Delane Sims turned her own experience as a single mother into a path for other women and families.

Delane Sims, Founder and Executive Director

Delane Sims, an Oakland, California native, created DNNC Steps to Success drawing on her own experience as a single mother of four children.

The work began informally in 2000 and was formalized as an organization in 2016. In 2005, while serving as an outreach coordinator for the ACLU of Northern California, Delane noticed that many of the people she met who were serving time had grown up in single-parent homes weighed down by poverty. She came to believe that when mothers raising children alone get the help they need, families are steadier and children have more chances.

Delane also founded Delane’s Natural Nail Care in San Leandro, the teaching salon where participants train. Her earlier community work included Senior Moments, an elder outreach program she started in 2005. That program is now carried on through the Delane’s Natural Nail Care website and is separate from DNNC Steps to Success.

Mission

Why we do this work

DNNC Steps to Success provides vocational training, education and support to women and single parents so they can obtain and keep jobs with thriving wages, and improve stability and quality of life for their families.

Prevent hardship

Career training and emotional support help families avoid homelessness, poverty and poor health.

Build careers

Scholarships and hands-on training open a path to licensed, stable work.

Strengthen families

Mentorship, classes and community support reach beyond the classroom.

Our approach

Women helping women

We use a working nail salon as a teaching space and an entrepreneurial incubator. Both staff and clients of the teaching salon provide mentoring and support, so participants learn from women who have walked a similar road.

DNNC Steps to Success works in partnership with Delane’s Natural Nail Care. Participants learn professional skills alongside social skills, wellness and financial literacy.

Leadership

The people behind the mission

Delane Sims

Founder and Executive Director

An Oakland native and former single mother of four, Delane founded DNNC Steps to Success and Delane’s Natural Nail Care in San Leandro. Her nail salon was named Business of the Year by the San Leandro Chamber of Commerce in 2017.

Myeshia Jefferson

Assistant Executive Director

Myeshia came to the program as a participant and grew into a co-owner of the salon and a leader of the program. She earned a bachelor’s degree in criminal justice from Cal State East Bay in 2010, formed the consulting company Beauty Means Business LLC in 2012 to coach women entering the beauty industry, and holds a medical manicure certification.
