How nominations work

  • The nomination window is December 25 to December 31 (Pacific time) every year.
  • Tell us who you are nominating, how to reach them, and why they deserve to be considered.
  • Submitting a nomination does not guarantee a vehicle. DNNC Steps to Success reviews nominations and decides on recipients.
  • We may contact the nominee and you to follow up.
