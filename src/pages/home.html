Our founder

Built from lived experience

DRAFT · NEEDS APPROVAL FROM DELANE SIMS

Delane Sims founded DNNC Steps to Success after her own experience as a single mother facing homelessness.

Public materials describe the work beginning in 2000 and being formalized as an organization in 2016, with a mission to help prevent homelessness, poverty and poor health among single mothers through career training, emotional support and practical help.
