Donate with Zelle

Open Zelle through your participating banking app and send your gift to:

Zelle email

donations@dnncstepstosuccess.com

Before you send

Confirm that the recipient appears as:

DNNC STEPS TO SUCCESS

How to give with Zelle

  1. Open your bank’s mobile app or website and find Zelle (sometimes under “Send money” or “Transfer”).
  2. choose to send money using an email address, and enter donations@dnncstepstosuccess.com.
  3. Check that the recipient name shows DNNC STEPS TO SUCCESS.
  4. Enter your gift amount and send it.

Zelle gifts are one-time transfers made through your own bank. This page does not set up automatic recurring gifts, and receipts are not promised automatically. To request a gift acknowledgment, email delane@delanesnails.com with your name and the date and amount of your gift. Not every bank offers Zelle.

  • Training scholarships for women and single parents
  • Mentoring and classes
  • Practical help for families in need
