# Donation Acknowledgment Process (Zelle) - DRAFT for Delane's approval

Zelle transfers do not produce the organization's tax acknowledgment. The organization issues it.

## Steps
1. **Record each gift** from the bank's Zelle notification or statement: date, amount, donor name as shown, and any note. Keep this log private (never in the website repo).
2. **Match requests.** Donors may email delane@delanesnails.com with name, date and amount. Match to the log before replying.
3. **Send an acknowledgment** within an agreed time (suggest 14 days) from a controlled organization email.
4. **Keep a numbered copy** of every acknowledgment with the log.
5. **Review monthly:** reconcile the log with bank statements.

## Acknowledgment letter should contain (wording requires approval)
- Legal name: DNNC STEPS TO SUCCESS, INC; address: 333 Estudillo Ave, Suite 204, San Leandro, CA 94577
- Donor name, date and amount received
- Whether any goods or services were provided in return
- A tax statement ONLY after IRS records confirm the legal name, EIN 84-4849258 and current eligibility, and the language is approved. Until then the letter thanks the donor without tax claims.
