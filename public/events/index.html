Events and community history

Gatherings that carry the mission

Since 2017, galas, award celebrations, community giving and vehicle giveaways have brought neighbors, mentors and supporters together for women and single parents.

Annual galas

Honoring mothers and community leaders

Our galas celebrate women and families and raise support for the program. Honorees have included a Mom of the Year, a Mom in Politics, a Business Mom of the Year and industry and partner awards.

  1. 2017

    First annual gala

    DNNC Steps to Success hosts its first annual gala and thanks the community for an outstanding turnout.

  2. 2018

    Second annual gala

    Held at the San Leandro Senior Center on November 1, with a program of award presentations. An etiquette class and a brunch at a local restaurant gave program participants a chance to practice their new manicure skills in the weeks around it.

  3. 2019

    Gala and awards

    Awards and recognition celebrated leadership in public service, nail education and motherhood.

  4. 2023

    Gala

    The gala is held on November 11, 2023.

Vehicle giveaways

A set of wheels for a family

Vehicle giveaways are among our proudest traditions. A reliable car helps a parent get to work, school and appointments. Local partners have helped make giveaways possible.
