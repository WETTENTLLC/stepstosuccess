Our founder

Built from lived experience

Delane Sims, an Oakland native, founded DNNC Steps to Success drawing on her own experience as a single mother of four.

The work began in 2000 and was formalized as an organization in 2016. Its purpose is to give women and single parents a path to a profession, a circle of support and a more stable future for their children.

Programs

Training for a career. Mentors for the journey.

Vocational training

Scholarship-supported training toward a professional license in the beauty industry.

  • Manicurist and cosmetology pathways
  • Medical pedicurist training
  • Internship and job-readiness training
  • State license exam preparation
  • Support toward employment or salon ownership

Mentorship and support

Training works best alongside people who believe in you.

  • Mentoring and professional guidance
  • Classes in financial literacy, etiquette, nutrition, parenting and more
  • A lasting community: once a Steps to Success family member, always one
