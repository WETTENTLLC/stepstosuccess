Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to answer you, and we do not sell personal information.

Information you send us

Our inquiry forms ask only for what we need to reply: your name, a way to contact you, and your message. Please do not include ID numbers, financial details or health information.

How we use it

We use what you send only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Donations

Gifts are completed on our payment provider’s secure pages. This website does not see or store card details, and receipts are issued by the payment provider.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising trackers.

Questions

To ask about your information, email delane@delanesnails.com or write to DNNC Steps to Success, 333 Estudillo Ave, Suite 204, San Leandro, CA 94577.
